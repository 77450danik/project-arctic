# The overlay of the volume keys (Windows 11's, corners of 4) with what a
# program plays (SystemMediaTransportControls): run with --sound --disk
# out/test-local/tools.img (volumetest.exe starts the volume icon where
# stobject does not yet, mediatest.exe plays "Shchedryk"). The volume key
# brings the overlay up with mediatest's card; its Next and Pause go to
# mediatest, whose title says so ("Next 1", "Pause 2") and whose card then
# offers Play; the tray's flyout has the card on top.
sleep 8
key meta_l-r
sleep 3
type D:\volumetest.exe
key ret
sleep 3
key meta_l-r
sleep 3
type D:\mediatest.exe
key ret
sleep 4
shot o1-playing
key volumeup
move 562 710
sleep 1
shot o2-overlay
click
sleep 1
shot o3-next
move 526 710
click
sleep 1
shot o4-paused
move 640 400
sleep 4
shot o5-gone
move 1163 784
click
sleep 2
shot o6-flyout-card
